## ZERO-FALSE-GREEN TASK RULE

A task may be marked `CLOSED / VERIFIED` only when all applicable conditions are proven on fresh evidence:

`MERGED TO MAIN ∧ EXACT MAIN SHA CONFIRMED ∧ REQUIRED CI / CERTIFICATION PASS ∧ TARGETED REGRESSION PASS ∧ INVARIANT PROOF ∧ CLOSURE EVIDENCE ∧ PROJECTS.md CLOSED HISTORY UPDATED`

A stale SHA, partial run, skipped required engine, provider-bypassed result, or inferred success does not satisfy closure.

`حذف المهمة قبل اكتمال أي شرط = PROTOCOL VIOLATION`
